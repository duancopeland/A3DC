A3DC Genome Functional Annotation

This interactive Krona chart shows the annotated functions of the A3DC genome as nested rings. The center holds 23 broad functional categories, such as protein metabolism, carbohydrates, and respiration; each ring outward splits them into finer groups, ending in the 781 individual functional roles at the edge. Slice size is the number of roles in that group.

Open the A3DC genome chart

Click a category to zoom in to its subgroups and roles, or use the search box to find a specific gene function or EC number.

The chart is a single self-contained HTML file (about 350 KB). It works on a phone but is easiest to explore on a larger screen. Click the center to zoom back out.

Source on GitHub. Chart made with Krona (Ondov et al., 2011).
